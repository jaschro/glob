492,000 images of public-domain artworks are available for free and unrestricted use.

The Met's Open Access Initiative makes images of public-domain artworks and basic data on all accessioned works in its collection available for unrestricted use under Creative Commons Zero (CC0). Anyone can download, share, and remix images and data about artworks in The Met collection.
